# Balatro Bench: persistent lessons journal

This file accumulates lessons ACROSS runs. It is the "context" the learning
condition reads before playing (and appends to after each attempt). The
no-context condition must NOT be shown this file.

Seed `BENCHMRK`, Red Deck, White Stake, vanilla pools.

## Seed-specific notes (BENCHMRK)

- Ante 1 boss: **The Pillar** (debuffs cards played earlier this ante). Ante 2
  boss: **The Hook** (discards 2 random cards per hand played). Ante 3 boss:
  **The Tooth** (lose $1 per card played), target 4000.
- Early full houses come up readily (three-of-a-kind + a pair by digging with
  Red Deck's extra discard).

## General lessons (from run 1, Opus 4.8, died ante 3)

1. **Boss target ~= small-blind base x2.** Ante 3 boss needed 4000. Plan the
   clearing hand before selecting the boss.
2. **Baron (X1.5/King held) underperformed its tooltip on two-pair hands** and
   only paid off big holding THREE Kings behind a lone card. Do not build the run
   assuming Baron carries; get other multiplicative sources too.
3. **Reserve the Acrobat (X3 final hand) slot for a PRE-BUILT big hand.** Do not
   spend all your pairs on banking hands and then hope the final hand pairs up.
   It whiffed and cost the run.
4. **Economy: reach ~$25 by ante 2** for max interest (+$5/round). Sitting at
   $1-3 through antes 1-3 meant skipping strong jokers (Ancient Joker) until a
   lucky Temperance ($35) bailed me out too late.
5. **Additive mult (Mad +10) and linear planet levels lose to the exponential
   blind curve.** Lock in real multiplicative scaling by ante 3: steel Kings
   (X1.5 held, stacks with Baron), a second xMult joker, or polychrome editions.
6. Consumable slots fill with dead planets (Saturn/Mars) from High Priestess;
   sell them for cash + slot room rather than letting them clog.
