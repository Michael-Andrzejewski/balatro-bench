- **Final ante: 11** (won the run at ante 8, continued in Endless). **Best single hand: 1,196,167** (Two Pair KK99, level 16, Card Sharp X3, Throwback X2.75, Hologram X2.5, Polychrome Egg X1.5, Swashbuckler +91 Mult).

### What worked
- Skipping ante 1 Big for the Polychrome Tag -> free Polychrome Egg. Egg + Swashbuckler (bought ante 2) was the engine of the whole run: +3 Mult every round played, ~+94 by the end.
- The Duo in the ante 2 shop made antes 2-8 trivial with Pair / Two Pair.
- Telescope (ante 7) + every Celestial pack = guaranteed Uranus; Two Pair went from level 3 to 16.
- Hermit (x2) and Temperance (+$50 from Egg's sell value) when they appeared for $3.
- Ante 8 boss was Violet Vessel (300k): skipping both Small and Big (Throwback X2, Garbage Tag +$64) was the right call - two Two Pairs + a Pair cleared it.
- Hieroglyph bought at the first ante-10 shop: replayed ante 9 for three more shops, which is what made ante 10 beatable (Hologram 1.75 -> 2.5, +5 Uranus, Card Sharp).
- Card Sharp: with only 3 hands, "any Two Pair, then Two Pair again X3" covered every ante-10 blind in two scoring hands; a junk play works as an extra 3-5 card discard.

### Mistakes / lessons for this seed
- Photograph (and Steel/Glass/Ancient/Baron) multiply during the card / held-in-hand phase, i.e. only the base hand Mult (~4-17), before Swashbuckler's flat Mult is added. Worthless in this build - only joker-phase XMult matters. Wasted a swap on Photograph in ante 5.
- Sold Cloud 9 for Blue Joker in ante 5; fine, but chips should have come from planets earlier - buy Telescope the first time it shows (ante 4 shop) instead of ante 7.
- Took Hologram at ante 9; Standard packs were skipped all run before that. Hologram (or Constellation, same shop) taken earlier would have compounded much more. Magic Trick voucher (ante 6) + Hologram would be very strong.
- Rerolled ~$150 across antes 6-8 for little. Rerolls beyond the first 2-3 per shop were poor value; that money in Celestial/Standard packs scales directly.
- Ante 9 Big (second pass): played a "junk" high card that scored 18.9k and accidentally finished the blind, losing a X3 hand. Compute the junk hand's score before using it as a discard.
- To get past ante 11 this build needs ~X4 more: an earlier Hologram/Constellation, a 6th joker slot (Blank was bought in ante 3 but Antimatter never appeared), or Blueprint/Brainstorm on Throwback/Card Sharp.
