## Post-mortem
- Final ante 4, best hand 3,343, won=false, died to The Club.
- The route is an economy-first Kings ladder and it needs its scoring pieces in the order written. I broke the order twice: I bought Baron at the replayed ante 1 (slot 10 for $8) with money that was meant for The Duo, and I filled the remaining slots with Rough Gem, 8 Ball and Swashbuckler before any X-mult existed. Baron's X1.5 per held King multiplies only the base mult, before additive jokers are added, so at ante 4 my mult was roughly 7 x 2.25 + 11 rather than the 40-plus a Duo would have given. Pairs at level 6 with two Kings held score about 3,000, and the ante 4 boss asks 10,000 over three hands.
- Suit-debuff bosses turn off Baron for Kings of that suit. The Club killed the third King exactly when it mattered.
- Verified facts for the next coached attempt: Hieroglyph's replayed ante continues the same ante's shop queue (so Baron at slot 10 is reachable cheaply, but only worth it once scoring is safe); a fresh voucher rolls for the replayed ante (Telescope showed at ante 2 here); The Fool copies the last tarot OR planet used, so it doubled a Mercury for free from an 8 Ball trigger.
- Next time: The Duo before Baron, keep Jolly until an X-mult exists, use the Skip Tag money at ante 3 the way the first run did, and do not spend on Crystal Ball before ante 5.
