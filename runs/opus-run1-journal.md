## POST-MORTEM

### What killed the run
The whole build was ACE-DEPENDENT. Scholar + Fibonacci + Duo + Mult-enhanced aces made ace hands (3/4/5 of a kind) enormous (14k-70k+), but my NON-ace hands stayed weak (~3-20k) while blinds grew exponentially (ante 6 = 20k/30k). When aces refused to come on the ante-6 Big Blind (severe variance - only saw 2 aces in a full round of aggressive fishing), I had no fallback that could clear 30k. A lone pair of 6s (no fibonacci rank) scored ~165.

### Concrete lessons for a future BENCHMRK attempt
1. This seed feeds aces HARD (Cryptid copy of AS-Mult in ante 3 Arcana; multiple aces in Standard packs). The ace engine is real and can hit 100k+ hands. Lean in EARLY but insure against variance.
2. INSURANCE is the gap: needed either (a) a real 2nd/3rd xMult joker (Blueprint/Brainstorm to copy Duo, or a generic xMult) so non-ace hands also scale, or (b) heavily leveled Two Pair/Full House (buy Telescope voucher + spam celestial) so the FALLBACK hand clears without aces.
3. Do NOT sell economy (Cloud 9) so early - I was broke ($2-6) for antes 4-6 and couldn't buy the xMult jokers/rerolls that would have saved me. Keep ~$25 for interest.
4. Steel cards (The Chariot) + holding them = great multiplicative scaling; got 2, should have prioritized 3-4 earlier.
5. Skipping blinds pumps Throwback (x0.25 each) - a viable scaling lever if picked up earlier and skipped into.
6. Tactical: don't blow ALL discards fishing on the FIRST hand of a big blind. Keep 1-2 in reserve, and remember non-ace pairs are near-worthless late, so a "cleanup" hand is not guaranteed - budget for it.
7. Mad Joker does NOT trigger on 4/5 of a kind (only real two pair / full house). Fibonacci covers only A/2/3/5/8 - pairs of 4/6/7/9/T/J/Q/K get almost no mult, which is why my off-rank fallback hands cratered.
